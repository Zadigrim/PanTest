Okuji wireframes v1

low-fi exploration · 3 directions × ~30 screens · drag screens to scroll
What we're exploring. Three structural directions for two surfaces: Okuji (mobile/Expo, collector-facing) and Okuji Connect (desktop/laptop, internal — Designer + Employee Terminal). The mobile app's novelty is the passport-book metaphor and press-and-hold to stamp with GPS verification; Connect's novelty is a required two-step employee redemption (scan → distribute) for audit trail.
